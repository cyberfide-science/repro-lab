Reproducibility reports, one directory per target: `report.json` (written by `harness/diff_claims.py`, validated against `harness/schema/report.schema.json`), `report.md` (rendered from it), `run_transcript.txt` and `container_run.log` (the terminal output of the run and the container's own stdout and stderr), `checkpoints.md` (the harness checkpoints firing, demonstrated on a scratch copy), `fresh_clone.md` (the fresh-clone regeneration and its masked diffs) and `review.md` (the adversarial reviewer's findings and verdict). How they are produced: `harness/README.md`.
